How it works

Features. Each book has 8 scores from 0 to 1: fantasy, sci-fi, mystery, romance, history, non-fiction, literary and darkness.
Clustering. K-means with k-means++ seeding and 25 restarts groups books by Euclidean distance and keeps the tightest result.
Your profile. The average of liked books plus your slider values becomes one vector in the same space.
Ranking. We pick the nearest cluster, then rank its unread books by cosine similarity. Two picks from the next-nearest cluster add variety.
